# 🦷 DentMateHQ

### Building intelligent tools for the next generation of dentists.

DentMateHQ is the home of **DentMate**, an intelligent assistant designed to support early-career dentists in learning, clinical reasoning, and everyday dental practice.

---

## 🚀 Our Mission

Our mission is to make high-quality dental knowledge more accessible, practical, and easier to use for dentists at the beginning of their professional journey.

We aim to build tools that help dentists:

- 📚 Access structured dental knowledge
- 🧠 Improve clinical reasoning
- 🦷 Navigate common dental cases
- 📋 Follow evidence-informed workflows
- 🎓 Continue learning after graduation

---

## 🦷 DentMate

**DentMate** is our core project.

It is being designed as an intelligent companion for early-career dentists, helping them organize information, learn from clinical scenarios, and approach everyday dental practice with greater confidence.

> **DentMate — Your intelligent companion in dentistry.**

---

## 🛠️ What We Are Building

DentMateHQ is currently focused on:

- Clinical knowledge organization
- Dental education tools
- Intelligent assistance
- Evidence-based references
- Practical clinical workflows
- Tools for early-career dentists

---

## ⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

DentMate is intended to support education and professional decision-making.

It is not a replacement for a dentist's clinical judgment, professional training, local regulations, or consultation with qualified healthcare professionals.

🚧 **DentMate is currently under active development.**

More information, documentation, and repositories will be published as the project evolves.
